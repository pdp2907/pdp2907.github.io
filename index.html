What we actually audit.

Let us audit your traffic behavior and map the correct install attribution window, view-through attribution window, and re-engagement attribution window.

Every one of those windows feeds straight into your reported ROAS. Inflate the numerator with overcounted conversions, and your ROAS is wrong by the same margin — not once, but every reporting period.

Hours Days

This is the one that actually produces the overcounting delta. It's the catch-and-release setting: how long a channel gets to hold a lead before releasing it back to the general pool. Hold too long, and a channel keeps claiming credit for someone who already converted through chat-researched, self-driven demand. Release too early, and whoever touches them next takes the win — even if it was never really theirs to claim.

30 days is the common platform default — shown here, not endorsed here. There's no universal right answer, only the number that matches how your customers actually behave — which is exactly what the audit sets, corridor by corridor.

Your dashboards are both overcounting and undercounting — just in different places.

What platforms claim

Every ad platform runs on a fixed lookback window, set once, for every advertiser, regardless of how their customers actually decide.

  • Meta — default click window7 days
  • Google Ads — default, new Search/Display30 days
  • Apple Search Ads — tap-through window30 days

Across the platforms and corridors we've measured, the real lag between a click and a conversion sits far closer to zero than any of these defaults — which means a real share of "attributed" credit is being claimed for conversions that would have happened anyway.

What nobody claims

In the businesses we've analyzed, well over half of all downloads and in-app conversions can't be traced to a single ad click at all.

A growing share of that gap starts before anyone opens an app — someone researching a decision in ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini, then acting on it days later. No ad platform sees that conversation. No MMP tags it. We measure it directly, corridor by corridor, from real conversion data.

A measurement partner, not a threat

Full-service or paid-media-only, we work with agencies the same way we work with advertisers directly — as an independent measurement layer, not competition for the budget. It's the same zero-sum game you already play. You rebalance between Google and Meta every quarter based on what's actually converting. We're not asking you to do something new — we're adding unattributed as one more column in a reallocation you already run.

And it's a capability you can bring to your own client relationships — an independent finding your client's finance team can't dispute, because it didn't come from the agency managing the spend.
